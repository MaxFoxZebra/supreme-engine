Workspace

        
Everything lives in one folder you own. CVs and applications
          are plain YAML, one file each, and letters plain Markdown. Copy the folder and
          you have copied everything; keep it in a synced folder and it follows you to
          another computer.

        
SetupThe welcome and the steps from the
          first launch: import a CV from a PDF or LinkedIn, your name at the top of the
          base CV, how it prints, and an AI client.

          

        
BackupsA copy of this workspace every day,
          beside the app, the last fourteen kept.

          

          

        
Sample dataSee the app in use:
          about sixty applications in every state over five months, tailored CVs, cover
          letters, and the base CV in French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. It opens in
          a folder of its own, made fresh each time; your workspace is not touched, and a
          restart brings you back to it.

          

        
Version historyEvery version of every CV, letter and
          application, and who made it: you, an AI client, or a change made outside CV Studio.
          Kept beside the backups, and any version can be put back.

          

        
Folder

          

        
ApplicationsExported as CSV for a spreadsheet, or JSON
          for another program: the list is never locked in here.

          
          

      


      

        
Notifications

        
Reminders from CV Studio, as your system's notifications: before an
          interview, and on the morning a follow-up is due. Off until you turn them on.

        
NotificationsOff.

          

        
InterviewsWith the time in yours and in theirs.

          

        
Follow-upsOne notification for all the ones due that day,
          and the ones already late.

          

        
New jobs from your scansOnce, when a search leaves
          jobs you have not seen.

          

        
Keep running when the window is closedSo
          reminders still come. CV Studio stays in the tray (the menu bar on a Mac); quit it from there.

          

        
Open at loginStarts CV Studio, in the tray, when you
          log in, so a reminder is not missed because the app was never opened.

          

        
Try itSends one now, so you can see where they appear.

          

      


      

        
Job scans

        
A search that runs every morning, in Claude or another AI client, can
          leave the jobs it finds here. You go through them one card at a time: right to add one
          to your applications, left to pass. A job you passed on is not offered again.

        
FolderWhere the searches leave what they find: any
          .xlsx or .csv spreadsheet in it is read, whatever its columns are called.

The morning searchA scheduled task in the Claude desktop

          places you want, and paste them into a new scheduled task.

          

      


      

        
Language & region

        
The language the app itself speaks, and the time zone it shows
          times in. Neither changes your CVs or letters: each of those has its own
          language.

        
Interface languageFollows your computer unless

CVs in more than one languageFor applying in more
          than one country: a base CV per language, kept in step with the first one. Off, the
          app never mentions languages.

          

        
Language of your CVsDates, month
          names and “present” print in it, and new letters are written in it.

          

        
Date formatHow dates are written in every date
          field, and the order you type them in.

          

        
ClockInterview times, in the fields and
          everywhere they are shown.

          

        
Time zoneInterviews somewhere else show in
          your time, with theirs beside it.

Appearance
          Follows your system unless you choose one. It changes the surfaces
            you work on: the panels, the forms, the tables. The window
            chrome stays dark and the rendered CV page stays white in both, because
            one frames the work and the other is the work.

          

      


      

        
AI clients

        
Connect an AI client and ask it, in plain words, to tailor your CV to
          a posting, write the cover letter or prepare an interview. With your mail connected
          to it, it also moves your applications along as replies come in. It works on the
          same files as this app: nothing to sync, nothing to upload.


        


        
What a connected model can do

        

          
render_cv
            
Renders the CV and looks at the page. A bullet stranded alone on
              page two, a heading orphaned at a break, a lopsided last page. None of it
              is visible in the source, all of it is obvious in the picture. This is the
              point of the whole thing.

          
edit_cv_fields
            
Changes single fields, and keeps the comments you wrote

          
create_cv
            
A new CV or cover letter, or a copy to tailor for one application

          
read_cv  list_cvs
            
Reads the YAML, lists what is in the workspace

          
write_cv
            
Replaces a whole file. Blunt, and it drops comments

          
ats_check
            
Reads the PDF the way an ATS does, and which of the posting's keywords it uses

          
design_options
            
Themes, typefaces and page sizes it may choose from

          
workspace_info
            
Where the workspace is and what is in it

        


        
And your applications

        

          
set_job_status
            
Moves an application along. Paired with a mail or calendar connector
              on the model's side, this is what keeps the tracker honest without you
              typing anything: it reads the reply, works out which application it
              belongs to, and asks you before it moves.

          
update_job_tracking
            
Interview times, follow-up dates, who is writing to you

          
find_job  list_jobs  read_job
            
Reads applications, and works out which one a message is about

          
job_alerts
            
The same list as Attention in the Applications view, read out loud

          
calendar
            
Interviews and follow-ups ahead, and an .ics file, so it can put them in your calendar

          
add_job
            
Adds one from a posting you paste, and refuses likely duplicates

          
set_company_logo
            
Points every application at one company to the same logo

        

        

          
These write to your files the moment they are called, and there is no
            undo in this app. They are plain YAML, so keeping the workspace in git gives
            you a real history.

          
Your applications are part of this too. A model can read them, move
            a status, record an interview and add one you never got round to logging.
            It cannot delete an application, rename the company or the role, or paint
            over notes you typed: it only ever appends a dated line. Those are not
            promises, they are missing parameters.

          
A status change is permanent. It appends to the history the funnel
            is drawn from, and there is no undo here either. The model is told to show
            you every change and wait, but that one is a rule in prose rather than a
            lock in the code.

          
Nothing here reaches your mail or your calendar. This app makes no
            network calls at all. Those come through your AI client's own connectors,
            they are only ever read, and nothing is written back to them, which is also
            why an interview time recorded here is only as fresh as the last time you
            asked.

About

        
A CV tailored to every application, every follow-up on time and every
          interview in your calendar, with an AI client doing the writing beside you if you
          connect one. Built on RenderCV and Typst.

        
Everything runs on your machine. No account, no server, no
          telemetry, which matters more, not less, once an AI is editing the files:
          your CVs and letters stay plain text in a folder you own, and the app and
          your AI client only ever touch that folder.
